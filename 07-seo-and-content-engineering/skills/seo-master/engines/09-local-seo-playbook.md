# Engine 09: Local SEO Playbook (GBP, Neshan, Balad & Hyperlocal Strategy)

Use this engine for auditing and improving local physical + online discoverability across Google Business Profile (Google Maps), Iranian map platforms (**نشان / Neshan**, **بلد / Balad**, **بهترینو**), site footers, and city/neighborhood search intent. Load target `brand-<project>` skill first for verified NAP and open local issues.

---

## 1. Canonical NAP & Directory Consistency Audit

Every local business MUST lock ONE identical canonical record across Google Business Profile, Neshan, Balad, Persian directories, website footer, `/contact-us/`, and `LocalBusiness`/`Store` JSON-LD (handed off to Engine 07/10):
* **Canonical Business Name:** Use the exact real store name (`فروشگاه ...`) — NEVER keyword-stuff city/price modifiers into the GBP title (high suspension risk!).
* **Canonical Address:** Standardize ONE exact street/corner/plaque wording across all platforms. If site footer and Neshan disagree (e.g., corner vs between plaques), flag explicitly for owner confirmation before publishing JSON-LD.
* **Phone Discipline:** Verify exact 11-digit Iranian landline (`051-3xxxxxxx`, etc.) and mobile formats (`09xxxxxxxxx`). NEVER publish broken/incomplete landlines or unverified numbers in new copy or schema.
* **Opening Hours:** Sync verified weekday and Friday/holiday hours across GBP, Neshan, Balad, site header/footer, and schema.
* **Primary Category:** Pick the tightest exact GBP category (`Butcher shop`, `Coffee roasters`, `Optician`, `Furniture store`, `Motorcycle parts store`, `Plastic surgery clinic`).

---

## 2. Entity Disambiguation & Namesake Protection
* Audit external map directories (`نشان`, `بلد`, `بهترینو`, Google Maps) for third-party listings sharing the same surname or brand word in other neighborhoods.
* **NEVER** claim multi-branch status or link to unverified map pins unless explicitly confirmed by the business owner.
* Anchor the real store clearly with its verified street/corridor name so users and search engines disambiguate the entity cleanly.

---

## 3. Hyperlocal & Neighborhood Intent (The Anti-Doorway-Page Rule)
* **Doorway-Page Guard:** NEVER clone thin location pages per street/neighborhood (`خرید X در خیابان الف`, `خیابان ب`, ... x20 clones)! That triggers spam filters and keyword cannibalization.
* **Clean Neighborhood Integration:** Weave real, verified neighborhood coverage naturally into existing citywide Category Hubs, Delivery/Logistics sections, or local FAQs (e.g., stating transparently that residents of neighborhood X receive citywide delivery under the store's real SLA).
* **Geographic Modifier Hygiene:** Target city modifiers (`مشهد`, `رشت`, etc.) sharply on transactional pages (Homepage, Service Hubs, Category Hubs, Price Hubs) rather than forcing city names unnaturally into pure educational/scientific blog articles.

---

## 4. Authentic Reviews, Q&A & Visual Trust Signals
* **Zero Fake Reviews:** NEVER write fabricated 5-star customer reviews or astroturfing comments.
* **Authentic Map Photos:** Recommend real storefront signage, physical workshop/counter/lab/showroom hygiene, and authentic product photography — never generic foreign stock photos or AI-generated fake shop interiors.
* **Competitor Pain Differentiation:** Turn verified market pain points from local competitor reviews into positive operational standards (e.g., respectful service at any order size, transparent weights/pricing, accurate matching of online orders) WITHOUT naming or defaming competitors inside GBP/map profiles.
* **Community Q&A / Review Responses:** Respond calmly and helpfully to neighborhood inquiries with verified contact info and accurate shipping/hours policies.
